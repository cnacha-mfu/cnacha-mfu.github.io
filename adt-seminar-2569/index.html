Challenge the Status Quo

6 old beliefs we need to question

“Teaching the full curriculum means our job is done”
Whether graduates get jobs is our result too
ADT employment rate fell 3 cohorts in a row
“Knowledge lives with the lecturer”
AI explains content 24/7. Our value: setting problems, coaching, opening doors
“Good grades mean a job”
Employers look at real work and experience before grades
Seniority bias · TDRI
“Revising the curriculum every 5 years is fast enough”
Skills change faster than curriculum cycles; courses must adapt every year
WEF: 39% of core skills will change by 2030
“Our competitors are other universities”
Our competitors: bootcamps, online courses, and going straight to work
Thai births in 2025: 416,574, lowest in 75 years
“Banning AI protects standards”
Make AI use the norm, then assess what AI cannot do
If we founded this school from scratch today, would we design it the same way?

AI and Learning Behaviour

Is AI helping students learn, or think less?

What research is starting to show

−17%

Exam scores fell with unguarded AI, though practice scores rose 48%Bastani et al., PNAS 2025

83%

of ChatGPT essay writers couldn't quote their own essayMIT Media Lab 2025

47%

of student–AI chats were “direct”: get the answer, little engagementAnthropic Education Report 2025

61%

of non-native English essays wrongly flagged as AI by detectorsLiang et al., Patterns 2023

Avoid
Change to
Letting AI think for students; submitting work they don't understand
Think first, ask AI second; AI as a tutor that asks back
Grading only the final product
Assess the process: drafts, prompts, oral checks, live demos
Banning AI course-wide, or a free-for-all
Set a clear AI-use level for each assignment in the syllabus
Using AI detectors to judge misconduct
Design tasks AI can't do end-to-end: real clients, local data
Skipping fundamentals: debugging, reading code, writing
No-AI practice for fundamentals first, then switch AI on
AI doesn't damage learning by itself. Learning design that fails to adapt does.
